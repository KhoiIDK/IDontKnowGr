Who we are?

We are a technology company developing digital learning tools and platforms, seeking web developers and designers to support accessible and inclusive online education services.

Our Mission

At IDontKnowGr, our mission is to make digital education more accessible, engaging, and effective for everyone.

we aim to:

  1. Accessibility: Develop learning platforms that are accessible to students with different needs and abilities.
  2. Innovation: Use modern web technologies to create interactive educational experiences.
  3. User Experience: Design simple and intuitive interfaces that make online learning easier.
  4. Security: Protect user information and promote safe online learning environments.
  5. Collaboration: Work closely with educators, developers, and designers to improve digital education.
  6. Inclusivity: Support learners from different backgrounds and educational levels.
  7. Continuous Improvement: Regularly improve our platforms based on user feedback and technological developments.

Our Services

IDontKnowGr focuses on developing digital solutions for modern education.

  • Online Learning Platforms: Websites that allow students to access educational resources and learning materials.
  • Interactive Learning Tools: Digital quizzes, exercises, and activities that encourage student participation.
  • Website Development: Responsive and accessible websites for educational organisations.
  • User Interface Design: Simple, attractive, and easy-to-use interfaces.
  • Database Management: Systems for organising student information and educational resources.
  • Web Security: Secure authentication and protection of user data.
  • Technical Support: Maintenance and improvements to keep learning platforms running smoothly.
